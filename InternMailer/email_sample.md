# Academic Outreach Email Sample

## Subject Line
**Subject:** Your recent work on [PUBLICATION TITLE] - Brief collaboration inquiry

## Email Body

Dear Dr. [RECIPIENT_NAME],

I hope this email finds you well. I recently read your paper "[PUBLICATION_TITLE]" published in [JOURNAL_NAME], and I was particularly impressed by your findings on [SPECIFIC_ASPECT_OF_RESEARCH]. Your approach to [METHODOLOGY/CONCEPT] aligns closely with my current research interests in [YOUR_RESEARCH_AREA].

I am [YOUR_NAME], a [YOUR_POSITION] at [YOUR_INSTITUTION], focusing on [BRIEF_DESCRIPTION_OF_YOUR_WORK]. I believe there may be valuable synergies between our research directions, particularly regarding [SPECIFIC_CONNECTION_POINT].

Would you be open to a brief 15-20 minute conversation to discuss potential collaboration opportunities? I'm happy to work around your schedule and can meet via video call at your convenience.

Thank you for your time and consideration. I look forward to hearing from you.

Best regards,

[YOUR_NAME]  
[YOUR_TITLE]  
[YOUR_INSTITUTION]  
[YOUR_EMAIL]  
[YOUR_PHONE] (optional)

---

## Best Practices Incorporated:

- **Personalized subject line** referencing specific work
- **Clear opening** showing familiarity with recipient's research
- **Brief self-introduction** establishing credibility
- **Specific connection** between research areas
- **Clear, low-commitment ask** (15-20 minute call)
- **Professional closing** with complete contact information
- **Concise length** (under 150 words in body)

## Customization Notes:

Replace bracketed placeholders with:
- Recipient's actual name and recent publication details
- Your specific research connection points
- Your contact information and institutional affiliation
